Kalshi Trading Engine v2.9.0 — Model Error Diagnostic Lab

Research-only upgrade. No live/paper betting logic is changed.

New endpoint:
GET /historical-trading-backtest/jobs/{job_id}/model-error-lab

The lab reuses the saved frozen April–July diagnostic checkpoint and requires no historical rerun.
It diagnoses:
- projection error and directional selection error
- month, side, ladder, entry-price, probability, claimed-edge, adjusted-edge, projection-gap and confidence segments
- ladder × price, projection-gap × price, month × side, adjusted-edge × price, and confidence × directional-error interactions
- largest descriptive loss segments
- stage-level comparison of model probability, executable market probability and actual win rate

Directional projection error is positive when the projection miss moved in the same direction as the wager.
All interaction cells require at least 3 bets. Loss-driver tables are descriptive and overlapping, not additive causal attribution.
