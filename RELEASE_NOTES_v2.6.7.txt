Kalshi Trading Engine v2.6.7 — Checkpointed Historical Jobs

- Adds SQLite-backed historical backtest job storage.
- Saves a full replay checkpoint after every completed slate day.
- Restores queued/running jobs from saved checkpoints on backend startup.
- Resumes at the next unfinished slate instead of discarding completed days.
- Adds a lightweight active-job keepalive on Render to reduce free-tier idle recycling.
- Adds GET /historical-trading-backtest/jobs so the frontend can rediscover saved jobs.
- Frontend Check Status can recover the latest backend-saved job even if localStorage loses the job ID.
- Frozen MLB model, T-2h entry rule, quote lookback, calibration, guardrails, and portfolio rules are unchanged.

Persistence note: point HISTORICAL_JOB_DB_PATH at a mounted persistent disk for durability across full
Render instance replacement or redeploys. Without a mounted disk, v2.6.7 still checkpoints completed
slates and uses keepalive to reduce the idle-recycle failure seen in v2.6.6a, but a full instance replacement
can still remove the local SQLite file.
