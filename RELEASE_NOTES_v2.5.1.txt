Kalshi Trading Backend v2.5.1 — Raw-Edge Gate + Tail-Safe Calibration

Why this patch exists
- v2.5.0 shrank all probabilities toward 50%, which unintentionally increased rare-event probabilities.
- That could turn raw negative-edge long-shot ladders into large calibrated MODEL EDGE recommendations.

Changes
- Selects YES/NO using the raw independent model edge before calibration.
- Adds a RAW-EDGE GATE: raw confidence-adjusted edge must be > 0 for a market to become MODEL EDGE.
- Calibration can no longer flip the selected side or manufacture a deployable edge.
- Replaces symmetric shrink-to-50 with conservative one-sided calibration:
  * selected-side probability <= 50%: unchanged
  * selected-side probability > 50%: shrink halfway toward 50%
- Therefore calibration can only leave conviction unchanged or reduce it; it never increases a rare tail probability.
- Keeps 4+ YES research-only and the 15+ raw adjusted-edge extreme-disagreement research cohort.
- Underlying strikeout projection engine and confidence engine are unchanged.
