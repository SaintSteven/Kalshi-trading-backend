Kalshi Research Dashboard v2.7.0 — Model Correction Validation

Purpose
- Keep the production/live card logic frozen.
- Treat July 1–31, 2026 as the discovery dataset.
- Validate one preregistered probability correction on a separate June 1–30, 2026 holdout.

Candidate change
- v2.6.x calibrated selected-side probability remains the starting point.
- v2.7 candidate applies one global affine reliability correction:
    p27 = min(p26, 0.01087202 + 0.62876989 * p26)
- Coefficients were fit once from the frozen July calibration buckets.
- The correction can only reduce probability; it cannot create new conviction.

What does NOT change
- Baseball projection engine
- Raw YES/NO side selection
- Confidence/QC engine
- 4+ YES research quarantine
- Extreme-disagreement quarantine
- Unit sizing
- Portfolio Selector v2
- T-2h historical entry timestamp and 6h quote lookback

Validation workflow
1. Lab > Historical Trading Backtest.
2. Tap "Prepare June Holdout".
3. Confirm Jun 1–30, 30 days, T-2h, v2.7 candidate = On.
4. Start one durable background backtest.
5. The same market/projection snapshot evaluates baseline v2.6.6 and candidate v2.7 in parallel.
6. Do not change the coefficients after seeing June results.

Decision principle
- July improvement is not evidence because July produced the correction.
- June holdout performance and calibration determine whether the correction survives.
