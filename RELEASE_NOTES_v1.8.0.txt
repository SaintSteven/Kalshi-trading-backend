CONFIDENCE V2
- Replaces the basic four-number confidence score with a transparent QC score.
- Adds confidence tier, recommended action, workload stability, workload range, risk flags, and positive reasons.
- Uses starter confirmation, role, season/career samples, lineup confirmation, recent pitch-count stability, and recent-change data quality.
- A numeric edge cannot become MODEL EDGE unless confidence is at least 68.
- Full paper stake requires confidence >=80 and adjusted edge >=10.
- This remains a research heuristic and must be validated through paper trading and backtesting.
