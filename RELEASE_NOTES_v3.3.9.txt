Kalshi Trading Backend v3.3.10 — Kalshi Market Subtrace

- Breaks the live Kalshi market stage into request/response/parse/pagination milestones.
- Persists trace snapshots to /tmp so the last recorded substage can survive a backend process restart when the instance filesystem survives.
- Prevents duplicate background trace starts by returning the already-running trace id.
- Scopes live market collection to series_ticker=KXMLBKS instead of paging through every open Kalshi market.
- Does not change frozen v3.1 scoring, edge thresholds, historical training data, or forward-ledger rules.
- Trace remains diagnostic-only with ledger writes disabled.
